# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a place where people can work together productively.  
The main rule is simple: be professional, stay on topic, and don’t make the community worse for everyone else.

You are expected to behave professionally, stay on topic, and treat other contributors with basic respect.

Do not harass, threaten, intimidate, personally attack, or deliberately disrupt others. Do not publish private information without permission. Do not make the project hostile or unusable for other contributors.

Criticism is allowed. Disagreement is allowed. Strong opinions are allowed. Abuse is not.

Maintainers may warn, restrict, or remove participants who violate these rules.
